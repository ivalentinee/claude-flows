* LLM Wiki: AI-Maintained Knowledge Layer

** Goal
Implement a Karpathy-inspired AI-maintained wiki layer — a dedicated
directory of Claude-authored knowledge pages derived from raw sources
(codebase, conversations, documents). Separate from human-authored
=notes/= (chaotic, organic) and from =designs/= (lifecycle-tracked).

Three operations: ingest (add knowledge from sources), query (search +
synthesize), lint (detect staleness, contradictions, orphans).

Key insight from research: AI handles bookkeeping, humans handle
curation. Schema quality determines system quality.
